vanderbi.lt Link Shortener

The vanderbi.lt link shortening service is only available for organizations affiliated with Vanderbilt University and is only to be used for official Vanderbilt communication.

If your department would like to start using this service, please open a help ticket with Digital Strategies to request access. In your ticket, please tell us how you will be using the shortened links (i.e. twitter, printed publications, etc).

vanderbi.lt is managed by Digital Strategies.
